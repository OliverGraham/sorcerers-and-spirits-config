Account & Data Deletion

Sorcerers & Spirits · Effective 8 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your Sorcerers & Spirits account and the data associated with it. Sorcerers & Spirits is a multiplayer dice-combat game published by Link Labs. It complements our Privacy Policy.

Delete from within the App

The quickest way to delete your account is in the App itself: open your account settings and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account and its associated data immediately. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Request deletion without the App

If you can no longer sign in or no longer have the App installed, you can request deletion by email. Send a message to og.singletonne@gmail.com with the subject Account deletion request, and include enough information for us to identify your account, such as:

  • the email address you used to sign in (if you signed in with Google, Apple, or email), and
  • your in-game display name.

We will verify that the request comes from the account owner before deleting anything. We respond to and complete verified requests within 30 days.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove the data we hold for it:

  • your account and its authentication record;
  • your display name;
  • your game statistics (matches and gauntlets played, won, lost, and forfeited; knockouts; attacks; abilities used; class-selection counts);
  • your achievements; and
  • your public leaderboard entry.

The copy of your account identifier and display name cached on your device is cleared when you sign out or delete your account from within the App.

What is retained, and for how long

Crash and diagnostic reports collected through Google Firebase Crashlytics are retained only temporarily and are automatically deleted within 90 days under Firebase's standard retention period. We may also retain information where required to meet a legal obligation.
