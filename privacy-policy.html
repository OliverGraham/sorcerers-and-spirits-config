Privacy Policy

Sorcerers & Spirits · Effective 7 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Sorcerers & Spirits mobile game ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who we are

Sorcerers & Spirits is a multiplayer dice-combat game published by Link Labs. For any privacy question or request, contact us at og.singletonne@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Account and authentication

To play you need an account, which we create and manage through Google Firebase Authentication. Depending on how you sign in, this involves:

  • Guest (anonymous) sign-in — Firebase issues a random account identifier. No name, email, or other personal detail is required.
  • Sign in with Google — Google returns an authentication token that Firebase uses to establish your account. We receive an account identifier; we do not store your Google password.
  • Sign in with Apple — Apple returns an authentication token that Firebase uses to establish your account. We receive an account identifier; we do not store your Apple password. You may choose to hide your email via Apple's private relay.
  • Email and password — if you register with an email address, Firebase stores your email and a secured credential so you can sign back in. We never see your password in plain text.

Profile and gameplay data

We store the following, keyed to your account identifier, in Google Firebase (Firestore and the Realtime Database):

  • Display name — a name you choose, shown to other players.
  • Game statistics — counts such as matches and gauntlets played, won, lost, and forfeited; knockouts dealt; attacks made; abilities used; and how often each class was selected, tracked overall and per character class.
  • Achievements — which in-game achievements you have unlocked and when.

Multiplayer and presence data

Because the game is multiplayer, while you are in a shared tavern or arena your display name and live match state (such as your presence and your actions during combat) are shared through Firebase with the other players in that session. Your display name together with your rating and win totals also appears on a public leaderboard visible to other players.

Data stored on your device

The App caches your account identifier and display name locally so you stay signed in between launches. This is removed when you sign out or delete your account.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics, and the App contains no advertising. We do not currently use any crash- or error-reporting service; if that changes, this policy and our store data-safety declarations will be updated first.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and maintain your account and keep you signed in.
  • To run multiplayer matches and synchronise game state between players.
  • To record your statistics and achievements and to display leaderboards.
  • To keep the service working, secure, and free of abuse.

4. How your information is shared

  • With other players — your display name, in-match actions and presence, and your leaderboard standing (rating and win totals) are visible to other players, as described above.
  • With our service provider — Google Firebase stores and processes account, profile, and game data on our behalf. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own privacy terms.
  • For legal reasons — we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or the service.
  • We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and associated data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account and its associated profile, statistics, and achievements at any time from within the App (account settings → delete account). You may also request deletion by emailing og.singletonne@gmail.com; we will verify your request and remove your data. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Account credentials and data are handled through Google Firebase, which encrypts data in transit and at rest. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. International transfers

Your information is processed on Google Firebase infrastructure and may be stored and processed in countries other than your own, including the United States.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about this policy or your data: og.singletonne@gmail.com.
